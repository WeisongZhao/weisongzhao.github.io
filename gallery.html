RIED

OverviewPaperCodeGallery

System:
Reaction-luminescence super-resolution imaging.
Camera:
EMCCD camera (iXon Ultra 897, Andor).
Description:
RIED combines entropy-weighted correlation with deconvolution to reconstruct super-resolution ECL, CL, and BL images at approximately 100 nm resolution, without external light excitation.
